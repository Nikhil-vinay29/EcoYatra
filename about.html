About EcoYatra

EcoYatra is a smart digital platform designed to promote eco-tourism and cultural tourism across India. Our website helps travelers explore the beauty of India’s natural landscapes, wildlife, heritage sites, and local cultures in a sustainable way.

Users can select any state in India and discover popular tourist destinations along with detailed information such as location, travel budget, nearby hotels, food places, and visitor feedback.

Our Mission

Our mission is to encourage responsible tourism by helping travelers discover eco-friendly destinations while supporting local communities, traditions, and nature conservation.

Features of EcoYatra

  • Explore tourist destinations from all 28 states of India
  • Detailed place information with images and location
  • Estimated travel budget
  • Nearby hotels and food places
  • User ratings and feedback system
  • Eco-tourism awareness and sustainable travel promotion
